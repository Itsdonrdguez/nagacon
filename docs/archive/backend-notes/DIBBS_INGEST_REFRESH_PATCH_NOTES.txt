DIBBS ingest refresh patch

Files:
- backend/app/api/scrapers.py
- backend/app/services/opportunity_ingest.py

What it does:
- DIBBS scraper route now converts adapter output into RawOpportunity objects
- Existing opportunities are refreshed when important fields are blank or changed
- Refresh targets:
  - source_opportunity_id
  - fsc_code
  - description
  - raw_payload
  - posted_at
  - due_at
  - url
  - solicitation_number

Retest:
1) Replace files
2) Restart backend:
   uvicorn app.main:app --reload

3) Run:
   Invoke-RestMethod -Method Post "http://127.0.0.1:8000/api/scrapers/dibbs/run" -ContentType "application/json" -Body '{"max_pages":1,"fsc":"6520","limit":10,"debug":true}' | ConvertTo-Json -Depth 10

4) Inspect opportunity 49:
   Invoke-RestMethod "http://127.0.0.1:8000/api/opportunities/49" | ConvertTo-Json -Depth 10
